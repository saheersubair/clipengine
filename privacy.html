Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

This policy explains what information ClipEngine (“the app”, “we”) handles and why. ClipEngine is a private desktop application operated by an individual developer based in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates. It is used only to manage the developer's own social media accounts.

1. Information the app accesses

When you connect a YouTube, Instagram or TikTok account, you authorize the app through that platform's official login. Depending on the platform, the app may access:

  • Basic account information: account ID, username or channel name, and profile picture, to show which account is connected.
  • Publishing access: permission to upload videos and their titles, captions and hashtags to the connected account, only after the owner approves each post.
  • Post statistics: views, likes, comments and shares for videos the app published, to track performance.
  • Access tokens issued by the platform, so the app can act on the owner's behalf.

The app does not access direct messages, contacts, followers' personal data, or payment information on any platform.

2. How information is used

  • To publish the owner's approved clips to the owner's own accounts.
  • To display post statistics and estimated earnings to the owner.
  • To schedule posts and respect platform posting limits.

Information is never sold, rented, shared for advertising, or used to build profiles of anyone.

3. Where information is stored

  • All app data (posts, statistics, settings) is stored locally on the owner's computer, in a local database.
  • Access tokens are stored in the computer's secure keychain (macOS Keychain), not in plain files.
  • The app has no central server and does not upload account data to any server operated by us.

4. Third-party services

  • YouTube, Instagram (Meta) and TikTok: the app sends videos and post text to these platforms through their official APIs when publishing, and requests statistics for published posts. Their own privacy policies apply: Google Privacy Policy, Instagram/Meta Privacy Policy, TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic (Claude API): to suggest clip moments, check clips and write post captions, the app sends video transcript text, a few still frames of clips, and campaign instructions to Anthropic's API. It does not send platform access tokens or account statistics. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

5. YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube account you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

ClipEngine's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections page.

6. Data retention

  • Post records and statistics are kept until the owner deletes them or disconnects the account.
  • Downloaded source videos are deleted automatically after a set number of days (7 by default).
  • When an account is disconnected, its access tokens are deleted from the keychain immediately.

7. Your choices and deletion

You can disconnect any account at any time inside the app or from the platform's own settings (Google connections page, Instagram “Apps and websites”, TikTok “Manage app permissions”). To request deletion of any data the app holds about your account, see Data Deletion Instructions or email zaheerzubair05@gmail.com. Requests are handled within 30 days.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children and is not used by anyone under 18.

9. Security

Tokens are kept in the operating system's secure keychain, the app only listens on the local computer, and connections to platforms use HTTPS. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect data.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
