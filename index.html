ClipEngine

A private desktop tool for short-form video clips

ClipEngine is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It helps one person turn long videos they have the rights to use into short vertical clips, review them, and publish them to their own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

What it does

  • Transcribes long videos on the computer and suggests short moments.
  • Cuts them into vertical clips with captions.
  • After the owner reviews and approves a clip, publishes it to the owner's connected accounts.
  • Reads view and engagement counts for those posts so the owner can track performance.

Who can use it

ClipEngine is not a public service. It is used only by its developer to manage their own accounts. It has no sign-up, no other users, and no advertising.
