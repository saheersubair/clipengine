Data Deletion Instructions

Effective 1 November 2026

ClipEngine stores data only on its owner's own computer. No account data is kept on any server operated by us. You can remove the app's access and data in either of these ways:

Remove the app's access yourself

  • Instagram: Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites (or instagram.com/accounts/manage_access) → remove ClipEngine.
  • YouTube / Google: myaccount.google.com/connections → ClipEngine → Delete all connections.
  • TikTok: TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions → remove ClipEngine.

Once access is removed, the app can no longer read or post to that account.

Ask us to delete stored data

Email zaheerzubair05@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion” and the username of the account. We will delete the account's tokens, post records and statistics from the app within 30 days and confirm by email.
